Assess Like a CMMC Professional in Five Days

A five-day workshop for cybersecurity and IT faculty. Work one fictional defense contractor through a full CMMC Level 2 assessment, see what the CCP credential could do for you and your program, and leave with labs, activities, and AI tools you can use in your own courses. Ten lessons, six CCP domains, one assessor mindset: describe, never advise.

⚠️ Regulatory status as of 16 November 2026. Check again before you teach.
  • 32 CFR Part 170 has been in force since 16 December 2024 and has not been amended.
  • The DFARS CMMC rule took effect 10 November 2025, beginning Phase 1 (Level 1 and Level 2 self-assessments).
  • Phase 2 (Level 2 C3PAO certification, due 10 November 2026) was suspended by the Department of War (DoD) on 13 July 2026. DFARS Class Deviation 2026-O0025 (Rev. 3, 3 September 2026) applies the suspension, and no restart date has been announced.
  • Still in force: NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2, DFARS 252.204-7012, SPRS self-assessments, and annual affirmations.

Exam Blueprint

CCP Domain Weights at a Glance

The CCP exam has 170 questions across six domains. Model Construct and Implementation Evaluation (35%) and the Assessment Process (25%) together make up 60% of it, which is why Days 3 and 4 carry the most practice. ISACA, as the CMMC Assessor and Instructor Certification Organization, publishes the outline; exam duration and passing score are not stated here. Check ISACA before you register.

About This Workshop

Built for Faculty and Program Leads

Who this is for

Cybersecurity and IT faculty and program leads who want to understand CMMC Level 2 at assessor depth, consider the CCP credential, and bring CMMC into their courses. It assumes working knowledge of security fundamentals; no prior CMMC, DFARS, or audit experience is needed.

How to use this resource

Work the lessons in order: one company carries all five days. Each lesson points to its practice activity and lab, and ends with a faculty teaching segment you can adapt. The transfer test on Day 5 is issued in session; do not open it in advance.
