CMMC Assessment Lab

Eight hands-on labs that put you in the assessor’s seat on one fictional company, Meridian Precision Defense Systems. Trace the clauses, find the CUI, clear the conflicts, draw the boundary, judge the evidence, score the gaps, and build the POA&M. Then prove the method works on a company you have never seen.

🗂️ Keep the case in view: every tool has a bar at the top that opens the Meridian Case Workbench in a side window, on the section that tool needs. The transfer test is the exception; it is taken cold, without the case.

Labs: 8Each: about 45 to 75 minCase: Meridian (fictional)Capstone: 30-min transfer testOutput: Print or PDF

How the lab works

Assess one company, start to finish

Open the case

Every lab draws on the same fictional defense contractor. The Meridian workbench holds its contracts, people, network, and seeded gaps.

Make the call

Each lab asks for a determination, a citation to a primary source, and what would change your answer.

Check the model answer

Reveal the model reasoning item by item after you commit. A short rubric lets you check your own written work.

Print the deliverable

Each lab prints as a numbered deliverable (D1 to D9). Answers save in this browser only; a progress code moves them to another machine.

For faculty

Using the Assessment Lab in your courses

  • As graded submissions: students print each lab to PDF and upload it. The printout shows their determinations, citations, written reasoning, and self-checked rubric.
  • Commit before reveal: ask students to finish a lab before they open any model answer. Count only the work done before the reveal.
  • Team mode: one student drives the tool while the team decides. The team submits one printout with every member’s name.
  • The transfer test: its key is in the page, locked until the 30-minute clock stops. A browser page cannot truly hide an answer, so keep the link private and issue the test in session.
  • Privacy: every lab runs locally in the browser, with no installs or accounts. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Currency: the labs teach the rules as written. Check the regulatory status notice on the home page before you teach.
